## Release gates

**Foundation gate:** both reviewers inspect this plan and Task 01 results. Privacy/authority/coverage rules are explicit, baseline capabilities remain honestly untested, and no secrets/private candidate facts enter Git.

**Contract gate:** synthetic cases cover missing evidence, expired calls, shared names, hidden units, pagination, teaching/Master's support, sparse CV, aspirations outside a CV, changed interests and candidate leakage. Invalid records fail deterministic checks.

**Investigation MVP gate:** two independently audited institutional pilots, no unexplained known reference misses, no known false merges, all actionable calls traceable and fresh, safe restore and resumption on both devices, correct private profile/query versions. Coverage-partial pilots can test mechanics but cannot establish successful full coverage.

**Reusable workflow gate:** baseline versus AOIS comparisons, different synthetic profiles and at least one different-discipline fixture show the core is not a fixed persona. Production never loads expected professor lists. Qualify unsupported fields and clients.

**Distribution gate:** owner-selected AOIS license; exact upstream licenses/notices; no unreviewed copied code; published compatibility matrix; versioned source/resources; clean install/upgrade and privacy tests. Public repository documentation is not an installable release.

**Enrichment gate:** each adapter improves verified recall or resolves a measured gap; its current authentication/rate/cost/retention policies and bounded errors are tested. Semantic Scholar and paid literature services remain optional.

**Action gate:** approved private facts, verified identity/contact, inspected anchors, factual draft, current official requirements and explicit action authorization. Do not infer permission from preparation.

**Monitoring gate:** successful limited-scope baselines, one scheduler and owner, durable state without local machines, semantic/replay tests, three reviewed dry runs and explicit weekly activation. No source failure is silently reported as no opportunity.
