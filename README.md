# Academic Opportunity Intelligence

An AI-assisted research-career intelligence system for systematically discovering academic opportunities, researchers, supervisors, funding routes, and future research connections.

The project uses reusable Codex/ChatGPT skills to exhaustively investigate universities across all relevant departments, research centres, institutes, laboratories, projects, and academic units. It is designed to identify not only advertised PhD positions, but also potential supervisors, research assistants, funded Master's opportunities, graduate assistantships, scholarships, tuition waivers, research fellowships, project-funded roles, historical vacancies, and other signals of possible future opportunities.

A core principle of the project is that **researcher discovery is separate from vacancy discovery**. Relevant professors and researchers remain important even when they do not currently advertise a position. Past vacancies, previous funded projects, grants, supervision history, and related publications are preserved as evidence of research interest and potential future funding.

The system places particular emphasis on exhaustive cross-department discovery for interdisciplinary areas such as GeoAI, GIScience, geoinformatics, geomatics, photogrammetry, remote sensing, Earth observation, computer vision, spatial data science, LiDAR, 3D reconstruction, geodesy, environmental modelling, robotics, urban analytics, and related fields.

The long-term goal is to maintain a continuously updated academic knowledge base that can support opportunity discovery, university monitoring, researcher prioritization, highly personalized professor outreach, and long-term research-career planning.
